The 7 Golden Rules

KPMG-standard research, enforced.

Every report follows the same seven rules used by Big-4 consulting firms when producing feasibility studies for institutional clients.

  • I

    Triangulation

    Every headline number from at least three independent sources.

  • II

    Freshness

    Prioritise data from the last 18 months. Flag anything older.

  • III

    Multilingual

    Search in English plus the country's local languages.

  • IV

    Official First

    Government and international orgs before commercial research.

  • V

    Benchmarking

    Compare to two or three peer countries in the same sector.

  • VI

    ESG Mandatory

    Every report covers Environmental, Social, Governance.

  • VII

    Primary Research

    Five to ten interview questions for key informants in every report.
