Why this skill

Built for serious deliverables — not bullet-point summaries.

Other AI tools give you generic summaries. This skill produces real consulting-firm output, structured the way KPMG, Deloitte, and McKinsey actually work.

📐

9-section consulting structure

Methodology → Country macro → Executive summary → Market → Imports → Regulatory → Competitive → SWOT → Appendix. The real KPMG framework.

🔍

Triangulation rule

Every headline number is cross-checked against at least 3 independent sources. UN Comtrade, World Bank, IMF, EIU, Statista, Euromonitor, and more.

🌐

Trilingual

English, Arabic (full RTL layout), and French — same report, choose at generation time. Add languages with a translation file.

📈

Real charts, not screenshots

Chart.js in HTML, vector charts in PDF. Findings as titles, sources cited, data labels on every bar.

🎨

Big-4 visual style

Navy + sky-blue palette, helvetica/cairo typography, full-bleed section dividers, country flag, KPI dashboard. Looks like KPMG. Costs nothing.

⚡

Two outputs, one command

Get a print-ready A4 landscape PDF and a self-contained interactive HTML in one pipeline. Share, print, host, embed.

The 7 Golden Rules

KPMG-standard research, enforced.

Every report follows the same 7 rules used by Big-4 consulting firms when they bill clients $50K+ for a feasibility study.

  • 1

    Triangulation

    Every headline number from at least 3 independent sources.

  • 2

    Freshness

    Prioritise data from the last 18 months. Flag anything older.

  • 3

    Multilingual

    Search in English + the country's local languages (AR, FR, ES…).

  • 4

    Official first

    Government / international orgs before commercial market-research.

  • 5

    Benchmarking

    Compare to 2–3 peer countries in the same sector.

  • 6

    ESG mandatory

    Every report covers Environmental, Social, Governance.

  • 7

    Primary research simulation

    5–10 interview questions for key informants in every report.
