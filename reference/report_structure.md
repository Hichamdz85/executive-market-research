# Report Structure — Detailed Section Breakdown

This is the canonical structure for every report produced by the `executive-market-research` skill. The structure mirrors the engagement deliverables of KPMG, Deloitte, McKinsey, and BCG. Every section has required content, optional content, and visual elements.

## Page-by-page structure

| # | Page type | Required elements |
|---|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Cover | Hero image, report title, product name, country name + flag, author, date |
| 2 | Table of contents | All sections with page numbers, section icons, author logo |
| 3 | Section divider — I. Methodology | Full-bleed image (left), section title (right) |
| 4–5 | Methodology body | Approach, sources, scope, limitations |
| 6 | Section divider — II. Country overview | Full-bleed image of country landmark |
| 7–9 | Country macro pages | Flag + GDP, population, FX, oil, trade, EoDB rank |
| 10 | Section divider — III. Executive summary | Full-bleed image |
| 11 | Executive summary table | Icon-based segments with key findings |
| 12–13 | Key insights at a glance | 2-page strategic snapshot with charts |
| 14 | Section divider — IV. Market review | Full-bleed image |
| 15–18 | Market review body | Demand sizing, CAGR, drivers, segmentation |
| 19 | Section divider — V. Import characteristics | Full-bleed (port, logistics) |
| 20–24 | Import body | Charts: import value, import volume, top exporters, domestic production |
| 25 | Section divider — VI. Regulatory | Full-bleed (signing/regulation imagery) |
| 26–30 | Regulatory body | HS codes, duties, FTAs, certifications, payment, restrictions |
| 31 | Section divider — VII. Competitive landscape | Full-bleed |
| 32–35 | Competitor body | Key player profiles, market share pie, pricing matrix |
| 36 | Section divider — VIII. Conclusion | Full-bleed |
| 37–39 | Conclusion body | SWOT, recommendations table, action plan |
| 40 | Section divider — IX. Appendix | Full-bleed |
| 41–end | Appendix | HS code definitions, sources, glossary, methodology details |

Total: typically **35–45 pages** for a single-product / single-country study.

---

## Section I — Methodology & scope of work

**Purpose**: Tell the reader what they are about to consume and how it was produced.

**Required content:**
- **Engagement context** — who commissioned, what they want to learn (1 paragraph)
- **Scope of work** — bullet list of what's in / out of scope
- **Research approach** — primary research (interviews, if any), secondary research, data triangulation
- **Data sources** — listed with hyperlinks (UN Comtrade, World Bank, IMF, central bank, industry associations)
- **Time period** — historical years covered + forecast horizon
- **Currency basis** — which year's USD, FX assumption
- **Limitations** — disclose data gaps honestly

**Visual:** Full-bleed image (containers/cargo/handshake) on left, section title on right.

---

## Section II — Country macroeconomic overview

**Purpose**: Set the macro context. A reader should understand the country's economic state in 60 seconds.

**Required visual elements:**
- Country flag (top of page or alongside country name in title)
- Country map snippet (optional)
- 4-card or 4-tile dashboard with KPIs

**Required KPIs:**
- Population (latest available year, with growth rate)
- GDP (nominal USD, latest year + 5y trend chart)
- GDP per capita (PPP and nominal)
- Inflation rate (CPI YoY)
- Currency + FX rate vs USD (with 5y volatility chart if material)
- Trade balance (exports vs imports, latest year)
- Top exports / top imports
- Ease of Doing Business rank (or relevant equivalent)
- Sovereign credit rating (S&P / Moody's / Fitch)
- If oil-exporting: oil price chart + crude production
- Key trade agreements the country participates in

**Required charts:**
- GDP 5-year trend (line or bar)
- Trade flows (imports vs exports, paired bars)
- (Optional) Sectoral GDP breakdown (pie or stacked bar)

**Source citations**: World Bank, IMF, EIU, country statistical office, central bank.

---

## Section III — Executive summary

**Purpose**: A C-suite reader who reads ONLY this page should be able to make a decision.

**Format**: A single-page table with **icons + segments + observations**.

**Standard segments:**

| Icon | Segment | What goes here |
|------|---------|----------------|
| 📖 (book) | Product definition | What the product is, HS codes, sub-categories |
| 📈 (chart up) | Demand | Market size, CAGR, growth drivers (1–3 lines) |
| 🚚 (truck) | Supply | Import volume + domestic production split |
| 🎯 (target) | Export attractiveness | Score (low/moderate/high) with 1-line rationale |

The icons must be SVG line art in primary navy, not emoji.

**Companion page**: 1–2 pages of "Key insights at a glance" with the most important charts (oil price for oil-economies, GDP trend, trade balance).

---

## Section IV — Market review (demand-side)

**Required content:**
- Definition of the product/service (technical, with HS codes)
- Sub-categories or product types (with mini-table)
- Total market size (volume in tons/units AND value in USD) — historical 5y + forecast 5y
- CAGR — historical AND forecast — with proper calculation, not estimation
- Demand drivers (3–5 bullets, each linked to a macro factor)
- Demand challenges or headwinds
- End-user segmentation (e.g., construction, healthcare, manufacturing)
- Consumer behavior insights (price sensitivity, quality preferences)
- Seasonal patterns if applicable

**Required charts:**
- Market size trend (bar chart, value + volume)
- Demand by segment (pie or stacked bar)

---

## Section V — Import characteristics (supply-side)

**Required content:**
- Total imports (USD value AND volume) — 5y trend
- Imports CAGR
- Top exporting countries (top 5–10 with market share)
- Country of origin breakdown
- Imports vs domestic production split (% market share)
- Import seasonality if material
- Logistics: typical Incoterms, transit times, lead times
- Distribution channels in target market

**Required charts:**
- "Imports by value" bar chart with finding-headline (e.g., "Imports grew at a CAGR of 2.76% from 2011–2014")
- "Imports by volume" bar chart side-by-side
- Top exporting countries pie or horizontal bar
- Imports vs domestic production stacked bar

**Sources**: UN Comtrade is mandatory. Cross-check with national customs data.

---

## Section VI — Regulatory aspects

**Required content (use clear headings for each):**
- **HS codes** — exact codes used in the analysis (table)
- **Import duties** — current % rate per HS code
- **VAT / sales tax** on imports
- **Trade agreements** — list relevant FTAs (GAFTA, AfCFTA, EU FTA, USMCA, RCEP, etc.) and what duty preferences apply
- **Standards & certifications** — required ISO, CE, halal, ASTM, local marks
- **Import licenses / quotas** — describe the regime
- **Restricted / negative lists** — note if the product is on any
- **Payment terms** — what's allowed (LC, advance, open account)
- **FX restrictions** — if any
- **Labeling / language requirements**
- **Recent regulatory changes** — last 12–24 months

**Visual:** Full-bleed signing/document image on divider.

---

## Section VII — Competitive landscape

**Required content:**
- 5–10 key player profiles (1-line each: HQ, year founded, revenue range, key products, geographic focus)
- Market share table (top 5 players)
- Pricing tiers (entry / mid / premium) with example brands
- Distribution structure (importer → wholesaler → retailer, or direct, or tender-based)
- Recent M&A or new entrants (last 24 months)

**Required visuals:**
- Market share pie chart
- Pricing tiers comparison table

---

## Section VIII — Conclusion & strategic recommendations

**Required content:**
- **SWOT analysis** — 2x2 matrix, 3–4 items per quadrant
- **Strategic recommendations** — table format: Recommendation | Priority | Timeline | Expected impact
- **Risk register** — Top 5 risks with likelihood × impact rating
- **Next steps** — concrete actions for the reader (commission Phase 2, identify partners, attend trade show, etc.)

The recommendations section is the most-read section after the executive summary. Make it specific.

---

## Section IX — Appendix

**Required content:**
- Detailed HS code descriptions for codes used
- Full bibliography with hyperlinks
- Glossary of terms
- Methodology notes (sample sizes for any primary research, calculation methods)
- Data caveats and limitations
- Author bio + contact

---

## Style enforcement

For every section, follow these rules:

1. **Findings as headlines** — every chart title is a finding, not a label
2. **Source on every chart** — bottom-left, "Source: UN Comtrade, World Bank, [Author] Analysis"
3. **No orphan numbers** — every number has units and time period
4. **No fluff** — if a sentence doesn't add information, delete it
5. **Bullets are full thoughts** — minimum 1 sentence with a verb, not noun-fragments

## Page templates available

The HTML templates in `templates/` provide these page types:

- `cover` — first page with hero image
- `toc` — table of contents
- `section_divider` — full-bleed left, title right
- `content_full` — content with header + footer, full content area
- `content_split_2col` — 2-column body
- `content_chart_left` — chart left, text right
- `content_chart_right` — text left, chart right
- `content_two_charts` — two side-by-side charts
- `content_table` — full-page table with header
- `content_kpi_dashboard` — 4-tile KPI dashboard (used for macro page)
- `content_swot` — 2x2 SWOT matrix
- `appendix` — small-text appendix layout
