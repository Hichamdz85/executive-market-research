# Authoritative Data Sources

Every numerical claim in a report must trace back to one of these primary sources. Never cite "AI analysis", "internal estimates" without a triangulation, or unsourced numbers.

## Tier 1 — Always trustworthy (use first)

### Trade & customs data
- **UN Comtrade** — https://comtradeplus.un.org/ — bilateral trade flows by HS code, all countries
- **ITC Trade Map** — https://www.trademap.org/ — complementary to Comtrade, more visualizations
- **WITS (World Bank)** — https://wits.worldbank.org/ — tariff + trade combined
- Country customs authorities — direct lookups for tariffs and HS-level data

### Macro indicators
- **World Bank Open Data** — https://data.worldbank.org/ — GDP, population, EoDB, inflation
- **IMF DataMapper** — https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/ — projections and historical macro
- **IMF World Economic Outlook** — https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/WEO — semi-annual forecasts
- **OECD Stat** — https://stats.oecd.org/ — for OECD members
- **Eurostat** — https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat — for EU
- **Country statistical offices** — always check the host country's national stats agency

### Sovereign / credit
- **Moody's, S&P, Fitch** — sovereign credit ratings (use latest)
- **EIU (Economist Intelligence Unit)** — country risk reports (paid)
- **CIA World Factbook** — https://www.cia.gov/the-world-factbook/ — quick country snapshots

### Energy / commodity prices
- **EIA (US Energy Information Administration)** — https://www.eia.gov/
- **OPEC** — for oil price benchmarks
- **World Bank Pink Sheet** — monthly commodity price tracker
- **LME (London Metal Exchange)** — base metals

## Tier 2 — Strong industry sources (use for sector data)

### General industry research
- **Statista** — broad coverage, paid for full data
- **IBISWorld** — industry reports
- **Euromonitor** — consumer goods focus
- **Frost & Sullivan** — tech and industrial
- **Mordor Intelligence** — emerging markets focus
- **MarkNtel** — emerging markets focus

### Sector-specific
- **HVAC**: ASHRAE, AHRI, BSRIA reports
- **Pharma/medical**: IQVIA, EvaluatePharma
- **Construction**: Oxford Economics, Global Construction Perspectives
- **Automotive**: LMC Automotive, S&P Global Mobility
- **Energy**: BloombergNEF, IEA, IRENA
- **Plastics/petrochem**: ICIS, Platts, Wood Mackenzie
- **Steel/metals**: World Steel Association, CRU Group
- **Food/agri**: FAO Stat, USDA FAS

### Trade press (cross-check, never as sole source)
- Reuters, Bloomberg, Financial Times
- Sector trade publications (e.g., "MEED" for Middle East projects)

## Tier 3 — Use sparingly with disclosure

- Company press releases — useful for product launches, M&A
- Industry association publications — bias toward member interests
- Local newspapers — for tender announcements
- Trade show / conference proceedings

## Country-specific sources (high-value)

### Algeria
- **Office National des Statistiques (ONS)** — https://www.ons.dz/
- **Banque d'Algérie** — central bank, FX and balance of payments
- **Direction Générale des Douanes** — customs authority
- **BAOSEM** — energy/mining tenders
- **Algérie Tenders** — public tender listings

### GCC
- **Saudi Arabia**: GASTAT (statistics), SAMA (central bank), Saudi Customs
- **UAE**: FCSC (federal stats), UAE Central Bank, Dubai Customs / Federal Customs Authority
- **Qatar**: PSA (Planning & Statistics Authority), QCB, General Authority of Customs
- **Kuwait**: CSB (Central Statistical Bureau), CBK
- **Bahrain**: iGA (information & eGovernment), CBB
- **Oman**: NCSI (statistics), CBO

### Egypt
- **CAPMAS** — https://www.capmas.gov.eg/ — central statistics
- **CBE** — Central Bank of Egypt
- **GAFI** — investment authority

### Morocco
- **HCP (Haut-Commissariat au Plan)** — https://www.hcp.ma/

### Tunisia
- **INS (Institut National de la Statistique)** — http://www.ins.tn/

## Trade agreements databases

- **WTO RTA-IS** — https://rtais.wto.org/ — all WTO-notified RTAs
- **GAFTA** — Greater Arab Free Trade Area, Arab League
- **AfCFTA** — African Continental Free Trade Area, AU
- **EU bilateral FTAs** — DG Trade

## Citation format

In the report, use this format consistently:

> Source: UN Comtrade, World Bank Open Data, [Your Firm] Analysis

or for charts:

> Source: UN Comtrade (HS 841582), [Your Firm] Analysis

For verbatim quotes from documents:

> "Quote text" — Source name, Year, Page X

## Forbidden practices

- ❌ "Source: Internet" or no source
- ❌ "Source: AI analysis"
- ❌ "Source: ChatGPT" or "Source: Claude"
- ❌ Citing Wikipedia as a primary source (use Wikipedia to navigate to the underlying source, then cite that)
- ❌ Citing aggregators like "Statista" without checking they actually licensed the data
- ❌ Pasting a URL without verifying it loads

## When data is missing

If a critical data point is unavailable:
1. State it explicitly: "Direct import data for HS 841590 in 2023 not yet published by ONS"
2. Triangulate: Use mirror data (partner-country exports), prior-year extrapolation, or proxy indicators
3. Disclose the assumption: "Estimated using 2022 data plus 5% growth, in line with sector CAGR"
4. Note the confidence level: "Medium confidence" or "Low confidence — for directional guidance only"
